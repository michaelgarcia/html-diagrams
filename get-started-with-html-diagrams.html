First principles

Content matters. What you leave out matters as much as what you put in. The clearer the picture, the faster people understand it.

Audience first. Start from what they know and what they don’t. Focus on the gap.

Invite feedback. Make it easy to accept, reject or iterate on that new information, visually.

Logical grouping. Group components semantically and logically.

One idea. One diagram, one message. Simple to share, simple to understand.

Visual language. Colors, line types and a legend make complexity easy to read. That’s the advantage of a diagram.
